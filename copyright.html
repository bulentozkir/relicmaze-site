Originality statement, procedural asset provenance, and third-party MIT notices for Godot Engine and .NET.

1. Copyright & Originality

© 2026 bulentozkir. All rights reserved for RelicMaze original code, chamber designs, narrative, and Dual 432 Hz synthesized scores (relicmaze.mp3 & relicmaze-phrygian.mp3).

Design, levels, simulation, art, music and sound effects: original work. Every chamber is machine-authored, solver-verified and replayed in two independent implementations. The soundtrack is a pre-rendered original piece, synthesized from code with no samples; the sound effects and every visual are generated in code. No third-party art or map data is included.

RelicMaze takes broad genre inspiration from classic flip-screen treasure-exploration platformers. No proprietary room maps, ROM data, character likenesses, extracted sprites, sound samples, or disassembled code from any classic or third-party game were used, and no affiliation with or endorsement by any third-party publisher is claimed.

2. Historical & Scientific Attribution ("Legend" & "Help")

RelicMaze includes two non-scrolling illustrated books: Help (14 pages covering gameplay, numbered HUD & toolbar icons, symbols, crowns, and accessibility) and Legend (17 pages paired with an 86-clip studio-voiced English narration and word-by-word timing track). Legend examines the real hill Visočica above Visoko, Bosnia and Herzegovina—separating public claims about the "Bosnian Pyramid of the Sun" (marked in amber as claims) from peer-reviewed geological and archaeological findings (marked in teal) and documented regional history such as Neolithic Okolište and medieval Visoki (marked in pale blue). Milo the Coppercap, Kabeel, the Keepers of the Meridian, and the Meridian Works are original fictional creations of RelicMaze.
