RelicMaze gameplay trailer

Exploring the pyramid

RelicMaze is an offline, single-player puzzle platformer for Windows 10 and 11. Guide Milo the Coppercap through the stepped pyramid, collecting seals and treasure to open each exit. Find nine Meridian sigils and the Heart to reach the finale. Hit a hazard and Milo goes back to the chamber entrance without losing his collected items.
