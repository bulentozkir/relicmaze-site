Exploring the pyramid

RelicMaze is an offline, single-player puzzle platformer for Windows 10 and 11. Guide Milo the Coppercap through a stepped pyramid: collect the seals in each chamber to open its exit, and find the nine Meridian sigils that lead to the finale. A hazard sends Milo back to the chamber entrance without taking away his progress.
