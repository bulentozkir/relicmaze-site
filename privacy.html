Privacy

Privacy Policy

100% offline play. Zero telemetry, zero accounts, zero analytics, and zero third-party data collection.

1. Summary: No Personal Data Collected

RelicMaze does not collect, transmit, store, rent, or share any personal information, gameplay telemetry, device identifiers, location data, crash reports, or usage analytics.

  • Zero Network Activity: The RelicMaze application makes no outbound network connections and requires no internet access to install, launch, or complete.
  • Zero Accounts or Sign-In: No registration, profile, cloud login, or online leaderboard is used.
  • Zero Ads or Trackers: No advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, or third-party tracking components are included in the game.

2. Local Device Storage Only

To preserve your expedition progress and preferences between play sessions, RelicMaze writes plain local configuration and save files exclusively on your own Windows PC under:

  • %APPDATA%\RelicMaze\campaign-save.json — current chamber, collected seals/sigils, opened latches, play timer, and score.
  • %APPDATA%\RelicMaze\settings.cfg — chosen UI language, audio volume, key/gamepad bindings, and accessibility settings.

These files never leave your computer. You may delete your expedition save at any time inside the game via Delete Expedition Save… or by deleting the %APPDATA%\RelicMaze folder directly.

3. Website & Distribution Platforms

  • Official Website (GitHub Pages): This static website uses zero tracking cookies, zero ad pixels, and zero analytics scripts. If you choose a language or toggle Clear View, that preference is saved locally in your browser's own localStorage (`rm_site_lang`, `rm_clear_view`) and is never transmitted anywhere. Standard web hosting access logs are governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Microsoft Store: If you acquire RelicMaze through the Microsoft Store, purchase and download processing are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Children's Privacy & Contact

Because RelicMaze operates completely offline and collects zero personal or technical data from any player of any age, it complies inherently with global data-minimisation principles (including GDPR and COPPA).
