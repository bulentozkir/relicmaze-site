Player Support & Field Manual

Installation, controls, save files, accessibility shortcuts, and troubleshooting for RelicMaze on Windows.

Windows requirements and installation

  • Operating System: Windows 11 or Windows 10 (version 1903 / build 18362 or newer).
  • Architectures: Native x64 and ARM64 packages (MSIX bundle and MSI installers).
  • Network: None required — RelicMaze runs 100% offline.
  • Single-Instance Protection: Launching RelicMaze.exe while already open switches cleanly to the running window instead of opening a duplicate instance.
  • Seamless Upgrades: Installing a newer build replaces the previous installation in place without duplicating shortcuts or deleting save progress.

Find RelicMaze on the Microsoft Store or watch the gameplay trailer.

RelicMaze save files and settings

Both the Microsoft Store / MSIX package and the standalone MSI installer write save data and configuration to your real user profile directory:

  • Folder: %APPDATA%\RelicMaze
  • Expedition Save: campaign-save.json (with crash-safe atomic backup campaign-save.json.bak)
  • Settings & Bindings: settings.cfg

Uninstalling RelicMaze leaves your save files intact so you never lose expedition progress. To reset your campaign, choose Delete Expedition Save… on the Title Screen.

Updating keeps your checkpoint, collected items, and previously cleared chambers. Best times and ghosts for routes affected by the new treasure requirements may reset.

Accessibility and keyboard shortcuts

  • F9 — Toggle Clear View low-vision high-contrast rendering on/off at any time.
  • F10 — Cycle through the four contrast-audited Clear View palettes.
  • U (or the toolbar ? button) opens the illustrated Help book. F1 or H opens the Controls card.
  • Tab or J opens the Journal. T starts a clean Time Trial for the selected chamber.
  • Ctrl + R retries the current chamber or Sanctum from its checkpoint. R alone resets a mine-cart board.
  • Settings → Accessibility: Scale the UI from 80% to 250% or turn on Bold Text, the Chamber Magnifier, Narration, Reduced Motion, Reduced Flashes, or Timing Assists.
  • Settings → General → Language: Choose from 30 game languages. Arabic, Urdu, Western Punjabi, and Persian use right-to-left menus.

Getting unstuck in a chamber or Sanctum

Use Pause → Show one move to preview an input from where Milo stands. After three failures in the same chamber or Sanctum, Pause → Assists offers a hint, half-speed play, or one move at a time.

The Golden Solution Bulb unlocks after 19 failures in Chambers 1–25 or 49 in Chambers 26–144. A Hidden Sanctum uses its entrance chamber’s threshold but counts its own failures. The replay does not change your live attempt.

Milo Move Out: mine-cart controls and hints

On most first clears, the onward door opens a mine yard. Drag a wagon with the pointer, or select it with the keyboard or gamepad and move it along its track. Clear the way for Milo’s red cart to reach the exit on the right.

  • A one-step hint is available from the first move.
  • After 99 valid slides on the same unsolved board, choose Show me the solution (K or L3). The game previews a solution without moving your live board.
  • R resets the board, not the chamber. Undoing or resetting does not erase the valid-slide count.

Troubleshooting and contact

No spoken voice for the selected language

RelicMaze uses installed Windows speech synthesis voices. If Windows does not have a voice pack installed for your chosen language, all spoken hints and descriptions automatically appear as readable on-screen captions. You can add additional voices in Windows Settings → Time & language → Speech.

Controller setup and remapping

Use a standard XInput or Windows gamepad alongside the keyboard. Under Settings → Controls, you can rebind gameplay actions, including fullscreen (F11) and screenshot (F12). Fixed menu navigation keys are not rebindable.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Windows system requirements for RelicMaze?

RelicMaze runs natively on Windows 11 and Windows 10 (version 1903 / build 18362 or newer) with native x64 and ARM64 packages. No internet connection is required.

Where are RelicMaze save files and settings stored?

Save files and settings are stored locally on your PC inside %APPDATA%\RelicMaze as campaign-save.json (with crash-safe backup campaign-save.json.bak) and settings.cfg. Uninstalling RelicMaze leaves your save files intact.

Does RelicMaze require an internet connection, account, or online activation?

No. RelicMaze runs 100% offline with zero network connections, zero accounts, zero telemetry, and zero ads.

What accessibility features and Stuck Helpers are included in RelicMaze?

RelicMaze offers four Clear View palettes, 80%–250% UI scaling, a magnifier, reduced motion, timing assists, and the Golden Solution Bulb. Narration uses a compatible Windows voice; when no voice is available, the game shows captions.

Does RelicMaze support controllers and custom key bindings?

Yes. RelicMaze supports standard XInput and Windows gamepads. Under Settings → Controls, you can rebind gameplay actions, including fullscreen (F11) and screenshot (F12). Fixed menu navigation keys are not rebindable.
