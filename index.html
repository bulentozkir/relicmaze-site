PART ONE · THE LEGEND

Help & Legend

The legend says the Meridian Works lie under a hill in Bosnia, Visočica above the town of Visoko, which its believers call the Pyramid of the Sun. Scientists say it is a natural hill. Read the whole story, with everything that is claimed and everything that was found, under Help & Legend.

THE LEGEND SAYS

The Pyramid of the Sun is 220 metres tall; later papers say 270, 300 and even 368. The Great Pyramid of Giza is 146. Its four faces slope at about 45 degrees and point to true north, to within an error that one paper puts at twelve seconds of arc.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE SAYS

Surveyed, Visočica stands 213 metres above its valley and 767 above the sea. The figures quoted for the base do not agree with one another, and even older texts of the project describe only three well-preserved sides.

ON THE RECORD

For scale: the Great Pyramid of Giza was 146.6 metres tall when it was finished and stands 138.5 today. The hill Visočica, measured from its valley, is taller than that. Whether anything but nature built it is the whole question.

The honest summary: the pyramid is a legend. The valley, its history and the wonder of it are real.
